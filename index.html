About TopCal Inc

The Standard for Large-Scale Commercial Projects

TopCal Inc is a premier full-service commercial contractor headquartered in the United States, with a track record of delivering complex, large-scale projects across multiple industries.

Founded on a commitment to craftsmanship, safety, and accountability, we partner with developers, school districts, municipalities, and private corporations to execute projects that demand the highest levels of precision and professionalism. Our integrated team of project managers, engineers, and skilled tradespeople ensures every engagement is seamlessly coordinated from groundbreaking to ribbon-cutting.
